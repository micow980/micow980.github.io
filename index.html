06 Data silently corrupts, and no one notices until a downstream number looks wrong. Prevented, not just fixed

I trace silent type drift to its root cause, repair affected tables safely with a backup first, and add guardrails such as lint rules and a text-first ingestion standard that casts in the view, so ingestion does not break on a stray value.

Approach

From epidemiology to production data.

I started in epidemiology, building the pipelines behind some of the first U.S. COVID-19 case research, turning swab and survey data from 50,000+ participants into two million analysis-ready records.

That work set the standard I still engineer to. In health data, a silent error isn't a bug, it's someone's record. So I build systems where failures surface immediately, destructive actions are reversible, and every load has a single source of truth. Fast when it can be, safe by default, and documented enough to hand off.
